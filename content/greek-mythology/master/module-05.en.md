# 2. What did Prometheus do?

Very simply:

# he stole fire from the gods and gave it to humans.

Zeus did not like this idea.

At all.

Because fire is not just the ability to finally cook dinner properly.

For humans, fire means:

warmth,

cooking food,

working with metals,

crafts,

technology.

So Prometheus is basically giving humans one of the tools that allows civilisation to move forward.

You could say:

> gods had admin access.

And Prometheus decided:

> “Let’s give users permissions.”

Zeus did not approve the access request.

# 4. Prometheus today

If you work in IT, the name may be familiar from a completely different place.

Again, we stay honest.

# 7. Pandora opened a large jar

Hesiod uses the word:

# πίθος — pithos.

This is a large ceramic storage jar.

People could keep things like:

grain,

wine,

oil

inside it.

This is definitely not a small little box Pandora carries under her arm.

Think of a large clay container.

Pandora opens it —

and troubles and diseases that had not hurt humans before escape into the world.

Only one thing remains inside:

# Elpis.

Usually translated as:

**Hope.**

Scholars still debate what the hope left inside actually means — something good for humans, or another complicated part of the punishment.

We are not going to debate it here.

The main idea is:

**Pandora opens a jar → trouble comes out.**

---

# 8. Then why do we say “Pandora’s box”?

Because the myth went through translation.

In the sixteenth century, Erasmus retold this story in Latin and replaced the Greek:

**pithos — large jar**

with:

**pyxis — box / small container.**

And the new image worked so well that it stayed.

Now the whole world says:

# Pandora’s box.

Even though technically it would be closer to:

# Pandora’s jar.

Thank you, translator.

One small word change —

and humanity has been drawing the wrong object for centuries.

# 9. What does “open Pandora’s box” mean today?

Not simply:

> “do something bad”.

The meaning is usually:

# do one thing that starts many new problems.

For example:

> “If we start changing this old system now, we may open Pandora’s box.”

You started with one small task —

and then everything came out.

Dependencies.

Legacy.

Permissions.

Old database.

Some service from 2017 whose owner no one remembers.

And someone writes in Slack:

> “Does anyone even maintain this?”

# Pandora’s box is open.

# 23. Then why do people always draw him with a globe?

Because the sphere above Atlas gradually became represented as:

# the celestial sphere.

It could have stars and constellations on it.

And if a modern person looks at a sphere like that, it can easily start to look like:

# a globe of the Earth.

Over time, the visual image became mixed.

So today you can see many images of Atlas literally carrying the planet.

But the original idea is:

### Atlas → sky.

Not:

### Atlas → Earth.

## Sources for fact-checking — editorial version only

Prometheus’s story as a Titan who steals fire for humans, his punishment with the eagle and the regenerating liver, and his later release by Heracles are well attested in Hesiod and Pseudo-Apollodorus.

In Hesiod’s *Works and Days*, Pandora opens not a box but a large storage jar — **pithos**; troubles escape and Elpis remains inside. The modern formula “Pandora’s box” is connected with later translation tradition.

Sisyphus’s punishment, endlessly pushing the stone uphill, appears in Book 11 of the *Odyssey*. The modern English word *Sisyphean* still keeps the meaning of a task that cannot be completed.

The story of Midas’s golden touch, including food and wine turning to gold and his later request to lose the gift, is told in detail by Ovid. The modern expression *Midas touch* is now usually positive and means the ability to create financial success.

The famous version of Narcissus falling in love with his own reflection in water is told in detail in Ovid’s *Metamorphoses*.

Ancient tradition describes Atlas as a Titan carrying the heavens or holding the pillars between Earth and Sky, not the planet Earth. In the story of the golden apples of the Hesperides, Heracles temporarily takes over his burden.

Prometheus is a real open-source monitoring and alerting system; the official project confirms the product’s purpose, but the official materials used here do not explain why the mythological name was chosen.
