## Chapter 1. Chapter head, drop cap and picture

The first paragraph of a chapter gets a drop cap when it has at least one hundred and forty characters. This is ordinary lesson text: one font, one size, one column width and left alignment in every Academy course.

The second paragraph is plain text. Links, **bold words** and *italics* inside a paragraph stay part of the text.

### A sub-heading inside a chapter

A sub-heading carries a small diamond and a short rule.

**A long line that is entirely bold becomes a pull quote between rules.**

**A short line**

# An accent line.

**Key idea:** a paragraph that starts with a bold label and a colon becomes a note.

> A quote written with ">" also becomes a note, just without a label.

*A line that is entirely italic is a lead line.*

- A list looks like this.
- Bullets take the theme's accent colour.
