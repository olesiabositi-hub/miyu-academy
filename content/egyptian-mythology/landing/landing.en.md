### They built for eternity. And almost pulled it off.

Thousands of years ago, on the banks of the Nile, a civilization emerged that learned to turn stone into immortality, death into another journey, and the gods into one very complicated family.

Welcome to Ancient Egypt. Here, even the afterlife takes preparation.

## Block 02. Before the Gods Could Agree on Anything

### First came chaos. Then came the gods. Things didn't get any better.

Every morning, Ra saves the world from darkness. Osiris becomes ruler of the realm of the dead after a spectacularly disastrous family conflict. Isis can do what even the other gods cannot.

Egyptian mythology tells stories of power, love, betrayal, and attempts to keep the world from falling apart.

And behind every myth lies something that real people once believed.

---

## Block 03. Death Is Only the Beginning

### You're dead. But don't relax just yet.

Ahead lies a journey through the afterlife, a meeting with Anubis, and the ultimate test: the weighing of your own heart.

The ancient Egyptians didn't simply fear death. They devised an entire system for surviving it.

It's astonishing how much effort humanity is willing to make for a chance at eternity.

---

## Block 04. The Architecture of Immortality

### The pyramids didn't build themselves. And aliens had nothing to do with it.

Behind the greatest structures of antiquity were architects, mathematicians, stonecutters, and thousands of people whose names history has barely preserved.

We'll discover how the Egyptians learned to build what seemed impossible, why pharaohs needed pyramids, and why real engineering achievements are more impressive than any fantastical theory.

---

## Block 05. Egypt's Last Mystery

### The most remarkable thing about Ancient Egypt isn't what we don't know.

Pharaohs' curses, hidden chambers, aliens, and mysterious writings. Egypt has inspired so many legends that the real history sometimes gets lost behind them.

But reality is far more interesting.

**Six modules. Thousands of years of history. Unforgettable gods, and people who managed to leave their mark on eternity.**

### Egypt outlived its pharaohs. Now its stories await you.

Empires disappear. Temples fall into ruin. The names of great rulers fade from memory.
